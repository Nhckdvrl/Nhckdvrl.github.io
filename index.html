About

Hi, I'm Xiang.

I am a master's student in Informatics at Nagoya University. My research interests broadly lie in natural language processing and large language models, especially reasoning, multi-agent systems, interpretability, and agentic learning.

I enjoy turning broad questions about model behavior into reproducible experiments, and building systems that help us understand where current language models succeed, fail, and improve.
