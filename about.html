About me

I work on natural language processing and large language models, with a focus on reasoning, multi-agent systems, and interpretability.

My research also explores agentic learning and the experimental systems needed to investigate these questions.

I like research that starts from a real tension or reproducible phenomenon, then builds a clean explanation around it. Outside research, I enjoy cooking, travel, photography, and games / anime.
